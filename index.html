    Voice commands

    Hold V while you speak and release it when you are done (can be changed to «always on» in Settings). Start with the team you mean – «Team A» (also «Alpha», «red team»), «Team B» («Bravo», «blue team») or «Everyone» – then say what they should do. Without a team name the order goes to the team you last spoke to.

    Speech recognition can run directly in your browser using local Whisper (works privately in any browser) or via the browser’s speech service. You can combine multiple orders in one sentence (e.g. «Ken follow me, Maya join Team B, Team B cover me») and reassign officers between teams (e.g. «Maya join Team B», «Ken switch to Team A»).

    You can also give an order to one officer by name: Team A is Ken and Maya, Team B is Jack and Zoe. «Ken, you cover us» · «Maya, go in» · «Jack, throw flash» · «Zoe, hold here». After that «you …» keeps talking to the same officer («you go in»). The officer acts alone until the team gets a new order, for example «Team A follow me». «Throw flash» at a door is thrown through that door; without a door it is thrown where you aim.

    «go left / right / forward / back»Moves that way as seen from you
    «go over there»Moves to the spot you are aiming at
    «follow me» · «on me»Follows you
    «cover me» · «watch my back»Stays close and watches your back
    «hold» · «hold position» · «wait»Stays put and keeps watch
    «stack up on the door»Lines up on either side of the door you are looking at
    «breach» · «clear the room» · «move in»Opens the door and clears the room
    «bang and clear» · «flashbang»Throws a flashbang, then breaches
    «open the door» · «close the door»Works the door without going in
    «peek that door» · «optiwand»Scouts suspects & civilians behind the closed door
    «C2 the door» · «blow the door»Plants explosive C2 to breach locked doors & stun threats
    «wedge the door» · «block door»Places/removes a wedge to stop enemy flanks
    «defuse the bomb»Orders the team to disarm an active explosive device
    «cuff them» · «secure them»Handcuffs everyone who has given up
    «hold fire» · «weapons free»Only return fire / fire at threats
    «report» · «sitrep»The team reports its status
    «Police! Drop the weapon!» · «Get on the ground!» · «Hands up!»You yell the challenge yourself – suspects and civilians may give up

    You can also reassign officers between teams (e.g. «Maya join Team B», «Ken switch to Team A») and combine orders in one sentence (e.g. «Ken follow me, Maya join Team B, Team B cover me»). In Settings, you can enable the optional in-browser AI Brain (WebGPU) for Solo mode so the squad understands free-form English, tactical questions, and relaying orders between SWAT units without any external API.

    How to win

    Every suspect must be arrested or neutralised and every civilian secured with handcuffs. Arrests score more than kills. Shooting someone who has given up, or without warning, costs points. Civilians without handcuffs run off when nobody is watching them.
