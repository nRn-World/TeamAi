    Voice commands

    Hold V while you speak and release it when you are done (can be changed to «always on» in Settings). Start with the team you mean – «Team A» (also «Alpha», «red team»), «Team B» («Bravo», «blue team») or «Everyone» – then say what they should do. Without a team name the order goes to the team you last spoke to.

    «go left / right / forward / back»Moves that way as seen from you
    «go over there»Moves to the spot you are aiming at
    «follow me» · «on me»Follows you
    «cover me» · «watch my back»Stays close and watches your back
    «hold» · «hold position» · «wait»Stays put and keeps watch
    «stack up on the door»Lines up on either side of the door you are looking at
    «breach» · «clear the room» · «move in»Opens the door and clears the room
    «bang and clear» · «flashbang»Throws a flashbang, then breaches
    «open the door» · «close the door»Works the door without going in
    «cuff them» · «secure them»Handcuffs everyone who has given up
    «hold fire» · «weapons free»Only return fire / fire at threats
    «report» · «sitrep»The team reports its status
    «Police! Drop the weapon!» · «Get on the ground!» · «Hands up!»You yell the challenge yourself – suspects and civilians may give up

    How to win

    Every suspect must be arrested or neutralised and every civilian secured with handcuffs. Arrests score more than kills. Shooting someone who has given up, or without warning, costs points. Civilians without handcuffs run off when nobody is watching them.
